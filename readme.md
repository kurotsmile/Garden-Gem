# 🌸 Garden Gems

Welcome to **Garden Gems** — a vibrant and engaging slot-style game where beauty meets strategy. Dive into a colorful world filled with fruits, sparkling diamonds, and blooming flowers, all fully customizable to match your style.

---

## 🎮 Features

### 🎨 Swappable Skins
Customize your gameplay experience by changing symbol themes anytime:
- 🍎 **Fruits**: Apples, oranges, bananas, and more
- 💎 **Diamonds**: Shiny and luxurious symbols
- 🌹 **Flowers**: Roses, daisies, and vibrant garden blooms  

Switch between skins freely to keep every spin fresh and visually exciting.

---

### 📊 Reward Preview System
Plan your strategy with confidence:
- Preview potential rewards based on your selected bet level
- Understand payout possibilities before spinning
- Perfect for players who enjoy calculated gameplay

---

### 🎰 Classic Slot Gameplay
- Choose your **bet level**
- Spin the reels
- Match symbols to win rewards  
- Enjoy dynamic visuals and satisfying effects

Every spin brings a new opportunity for big wins.

---

## 🚀 How to Play

1. Select your preferred **symbol skin**
2. Choose a **bet level**
3. Press **Spin**
4. Match symbols across reels to earn rewards
5. Adjust skins or bets anytime for a new experience
